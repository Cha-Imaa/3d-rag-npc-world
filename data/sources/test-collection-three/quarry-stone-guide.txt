A Guide to Greyfold Quarry Stone

This guide is invented test material for Loreland. Greyfold Quarry and everything described here are fictional.

Greyfold Quarry lies two hours east of the village, in a fold of the hills where the rock comes close to the surface. The quarry produces a pale grey sandstone with thin blue bands. Builders call the banded stone ribbonstone, and it is the stone used in the Millrace Bridge, the chapel steps, and the well on the market square.

Ribbonstone is soft when it first comes out of the ground and hardens slowly in the air. For this reason, quarrymen cut and shape the stone within a month of lifting it, while it is still easy to work. A block left out for a full year becomes so hard that it can blunt a chisel in a single afternoon.

Stone is cut in three standard sizes. A hand block is small enough for one person to carry. A cart block needs two people and fills a quarter of a cart. A keystone block is cut only to order, measured against a wooden template that the mason brings to the quarry.

The quarry closes every winter from the Month of Ice until the thaw, because water in the cracks freezes and splits the rock face without warning. In the year 452 a section of the north face collapsed during an early thaw. Nobody was hurt, but the quarry did not reopen until midsummer.

The quarry keeps a tally stick for every cart that leaves. Each notch stands for one cart block. At the end of each season the tally sticks are burned in a bonfire on the quarry floor, and the quarry master announces the total. The record season was the summer of 438, when 1,260 cart blocks left the quarry, mostly to rebuild homes after the great flood.
